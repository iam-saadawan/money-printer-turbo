## Layered method (from Part 4 on) — use this, not stick_story.py
- Why: the free pollen only runs `flux.2-klein-4b`; asked for whole multi-character scenes it breaks faces/duplicates
  people/changes rooms. One subject per image works: backgrounds (empty plates), single-character poses on green,
  object inserts. Code composites and animates. Better models need paid pollen (e.g. seedream-5.0-flash ~0.02/img).
- Files: `tools/layered_story.py` (assets / sheet / render), `tools/layered_render.py` (compositor). Script is written
  as Python in the episode's `build_script.py` (locations, poses, inserts, shots) → `script.json`.
- Shot format: `cam` = wide | medium:<x> | close:<actor idx> | insert:<id>; actors `[pose, x, depth, facing, [walk x0,x1]]`;
  `then` = `[word_idx, actor_idx, new_pose]`; `fx: punch`; `sfx: ring|click|door`. `split_after_shot` → parts A/B.
- Every prop the narration mentions gets an insert or a pose holding it. Keep each part ≤ 178 s.
- Review `_sheet_{plates,poses,inserts}.jpg`, delete bad images, rerun `assets` (regenerates only missing).

## Part 6 lessons (user rejected v1, 2026-10-31) — check these on EVERY layered episode
- Entrances: a character who "opens the door" must appear IN that door. Draw the door facing the camera (plate) and an
  `EDIT:` open-door variant; never walk people in from a wall.
- Costume continuity per scene: never reuse a bust/pose from another episode if the outfit differs (Leo: uniform busts
  from Part 4 in a home scene). Decide each character's outfit per scene before writing poses.
- Furniture: if a pose includes its own chair/armchair, the plate must NOT have one there (two armchairs; diner chairs).
  Two people "at a table" must sit at the SAME table.
- Everyone who acts must arrive on screen first (you/Brick appeared inside the truck); keep the mastermind (Walter) present.
- No insert/close-up held > ~3 s; open with a hook; add a complication to every set-piece; explain every character's
  choices (why didn't Victor warn Eleanor?). Countdown clocks jump per shot (story time), never race in real time.
- Review EVERY 1.5 s of the render before sending, not every 3 s.
- v2 was rejected too ("no one can tell what's going on"): flashback hook + on-screen timer confused viewers, too many
  threads for 3 min, cutouts never interacted. v3 fix: straight line, plan stated up front, real 2-person `frames`
  (shot cam `frame:<id>`, drawn INTO the location plate with cast sheets / approved pose images as refs). klein-4b
  merges people when a frame has 3 characters -> max 2 per frame, layer the rest. Characters must FACE what they act
  on (side-view poses for talking to a door).
